A map of the in-between

Places I’ve been,
things I noticed.

This is a growing collection of film photos from the roads, train platforms, quiet cafés, and borrowed rooms that have stayed with me.

Every pin is a place I’ve passed through. Every photograph is a small attempt to remember what it felt like to be there — before the next train, the next morning, the next turn.
